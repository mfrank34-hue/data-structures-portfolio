What is Data Science to Me?

Data science to me is using a combination of math, science, computer programs, analytics, and data visualization to create meaningful insights. Learning data science in a social and interdisciplinary context matters to help us solve issues like climate change, poverty, public health, and education. Modeling topics like public health are important to help improve treatment plans and develop new medicines. It can also help improve climate change and poverty. These can be life-saving for some people. This can help us connect raw numbers to real world data. Using real people can help give better and more accurate results. These results can help researchers, government agencies, and non-profits figure out better solutions to problems by having real world evidence. By using social topics and people as data, there are many responsibilities that come with it. Communication is important. Keeping people informed on what their information is being used for and having open communication is key. Privacy and protecting privacy is an important responsibility. Keeping data that is supposed to be anonymous, anonymous is important. Removing any names and identifying factors is important. Lastly, making the intentions of the data and what it is being used for clear is a part of responsibly using people and social topics as data. It is important when using real people and social topics as data to make sure it is done responsibly. After taking this course I hope to better understand collecting and analyzing data. I want to learn tools to ethically and correctly collect good data that can be used and be understood. I also hope to get a better understanding of ways to analyze this data to eventually be able to create a visualization that can be easily comprehended. I also hope to improve my coding skills. Overall, I wish to learn more about data science and to understand it more.
